I also prepared a small undergraduate teaching demo on model evaluation and data leakage:

[Model Evaluation Lab link goes here]

The lab is designed for an introductory data science or machine learning course. It asks students to compare a random train and test split with a group held out split, then explain why the validation design changes the claim they can make about generalization. I built it as an example of the kind of applied, reproducible assignment I would enjoy developing for students in a public liberal arts computer science program.
